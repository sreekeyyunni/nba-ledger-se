How it works. Each team gets a rating from its roster (Win Shares per 48 minutes times projected minutes, filled to 240 minutes with replacement-level players) plus its form so far, the same ratings as the Predictions page. Every remaining game is played with home court worth 2.3 points, a 1.5-point penalty on the second night of a back-to-back, and a game-to-game spread of 11.5 points. Each simulated season also shifts every team's rating by a random amount (about 2 points), because ratings this early are uncertain. Seeds 1–6 go straight to the playoffs; 7–10 play the play-in (7 v 8, then the loser hosts the 9 v 10 winner). Playoff rounds are best of seven with home court to the better record. Ties in the standings are coin flips, not the real tiebreakers. Long injuries count for the rest of the season; day-to-day ones don't. Data refreshes with the Predictions page.
