Tale of the tape

    How the series is simulated
    • Talent: each player's WS/48 (last season blended with this one, the same numbers the game predictor uses). Starters play up to 8 minutes more than their usual load, capped at 38; the rest of the 240 minutes go to a generic bench.
    • Stamina: every minute past a player's normal load costs him about 1.2% efficiency, and the strain grows from Game 4 on. Stretched players also get hurt a little more often.
    • Too many cooks: when the five's combined usage rate passes 105%, they cost each other about 1 point per 10% over, because there's only one ball.
    • Fit: the Play styles model scores how the five cover shot creation, shooting, rim pressure, passing, rebounding, rim protection and defense, compared with real teams' top five. Worth up to ±3 points.
    • Injury luck: the more games a player missed last season, the more likely he gets hurt in a game (about 2% a game for an ironman, 7% for someone who missed half the year). Injuries last 1 to 5 games. The opponent's rotation rolls the same dice.
    • Opponent: the team's rest-of-season rating from the Season sim (roster plus form). Players you pick are taken off their team. The opponent has home court (games 1, 2, 5 and 7), and each game lands within about 11.5 points of the expected margin.
