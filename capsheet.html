Cap Sheet

Every team's payroll, luxury tax, apron status, exceptions, cap holds, dead money and picks for 2026-27, 2027-28 and 2028-29. Click a team for every line item.

How to read it. Payroll = guaranteed + options (assumed picked up) + dead money + first-round picks at their expected slot's rookie scale + a minimum-contract charge for each roster spot under 12 (2027-28 on). Cap holds are shown separately, because renouncing a free agent removes his hold: "Space" ignores holds, "Space with holds" counts them. Holds use 150% of last salary above $13.5M, 190% below, 250% for rookie-scale players, floor $2.4M, cap 35% of the cap.

Cap numbers. 2026-27: cap $165.0M, tax $200.4M, aprons $209.0M and $221.7M (Spotrac). 2027-28: cap $176.0M (September 2026 projection), others scaled. 2028-29: +7%. Exceptions: room exception 5.678% of the cap, non-taxpayer MLE 9.12% (hard-caps a team at the first apron), taxpayer MLE 3.676%, over the second apron minimum contracts only.

Limits. 2026-27 payroll is Spotrac's cash total (includes dead money). Dead money beyond 2027-28 is not tracked yet, 2028-29 only counts contracts already signed (so most teams show lots of room that future signings will eat), and the tax bill uses non-repeater rates ($1.50 per dollar, rising each $5M) because repeater status isn't tracked. Two-way contracts are not counted. Sources: Spotrac, Basketball Reference, NBC Sports.
