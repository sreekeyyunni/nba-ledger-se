What this can and can't tell you. It only counts players under contract today, so later seasons shrink as deals expire: read them as value already locked in, not a win prediction. Each player's future value ages him on a standard curve from last season plus this season's first few weeks, so young players with a hot or cold start can swing a lot until about January. It doesn't know about future trades, free-agent signings, extensions or draft picks who haven't played. Payroll and cap status come from the cap sheet and stop at 2028-29.
