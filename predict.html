Tonight's picks

Every game gets a win probability from each roster's Win Shares, who is hurt, how teams have played so far, home court and back-to-backs. Picks lock before tip-off and stay on the record.

Power ratings

Points better or worse than an average team on a neutral floor, as of tonight. Roster is the Win Shares rating with tonight's injuries; form is how much a team has beaten or missed its expected margin, shrunk toward zero while the sample is small.

How the model works
  • Roster rating: each player's WS/48 (last season blended with this one, small samples pulled toward bench level) times projected minutes, filled to 240 minutes with replacement-level players. Converted to points with about 2.7 wins per point of margin.
  • Injuries: players listed out are removed; day-to-day or questionable players count for half their minutes. Cost shown is what losing the player fully is worth.
  • Form: the average of (actual margin minus expected margin) per game, with 8 games' worth of weight on zero. Blowouts are capped at 25.
  • Game line: home rating minus away rating, plus 2.3 points for home court, minus 1.5 for a team on the second night of a back-to-back. Win chance assumes real margins land within about 11.5 points of the line.
  • Record: "Live" picks were locked on game day. "Backtest" picks fill in games played before the predictor existed, using only results from earlier nights and no injury news.
