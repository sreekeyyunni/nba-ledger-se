How it's scored

Each week Claude reads the sources below and rates each team twice on a −2 to +2 scale: how its preseason outlook was framed, and how this week's results and play were written up. The team score weights this week at 60% and the outlook at 40%. Players get one rating from this week's coverage. Fan voices (Reddit team subs) aren't in yet. Early-season scores swing on one or two games, so read them as tone, not truth.
