Cap figures for 2027-28. Cap $176.0M is the league's September 2026 projection; the tax line and aprons are scaled from 2026-27 by the same ratios (tax $213.8M, first apron $223.0M, second apron $236.5M). Exceptions are scaled from the 2025-26 amounts: non-taxpayer MLE $16.1M, taxpayer MLE $6.5M, room exception $10.0M.

Cap holds are approximations of the CBA rules: 150% of last salary for players earning above about $13.5M, 190% below that, 250% for rookie-scale players, at least $2.4M, never above the player's max. First-round picks hold their slot's rookie scale (scaled from 2025 to 2027). Under 12 players, each empty spot is charged a rookie minimum ($1.36M). Dead money for 2027-28 comes from Spotrac's league cash table (Phoenix and Milwaukee carry the most). Two-way contracts are not counted.

Prices. Fair price = healthy production value (Contract Ledger "If healthy" lens), then per year +7% for cap growth and an age curve (+8% through 23, +3% at 24-26, flat at 27-29, −5% at 30-31, −10% at 32-33, −16% after). Max salaries use age as a stand-in for years of service: 25% of the cap under 27, 30% at 27-29, 35% from 30. Extension surplus discounts later years 8% a year.

Draft. Order and protections come from the tracker's 2027 pick map (projected standings, swaps and protections resolved by slot). The lottery button uses the real 2019 odds (14% each for the three worst teams). Prospects: Tankathon 2027 big board top 60, October 2026. Sources: Basketball Reference, Spotrac, NBC Sports (cap projection), Tankathon.
